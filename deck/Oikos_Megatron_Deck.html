Method, glossary and limits

Four steps, one funnel, and clear limits on what the data can say.

1Keyword listing and classification

5,838 keywords, 9 topics, 3 funnel stages

2SEO matrix (SE Ranking)

Share of voice per topic, Google USA

3AI matrix (SE Visible)

4Benchmark and recommendations

Competitors, SEO and GEO, priorities

Glossary

Search volumeAverage monthly Google searches, USA, last 48 months. Close variants and misspellings counted once.
Share of voiceA domain's share of the estimated traffic in a topic's keyword set (SE Ranking). Not additive across topics.
Visibility (AI)Share of runs of a prompt in which Oikos is mentioned (SE Visible).
Net sentimentSE Visible's sentiment score per prompt. Definition not given in the export.
Branded / unbrandedBranded = the Oikos name or a misspelling. Unbranded = category searches and questions.
FunnelDiscover (Moment, Recipe, Health), Evaluate (Ingredients, Testimonial), Buy (Product, Flavor, Packaging, Purchase).

Limits

  • SE Ranking exports are from 22 Nov 2026, from the old project (includes Triple Zero and ambiguous keywords). Re-export pending.
  • SE Visible: 111 prompts, one week, 7 to 30 prompts per topic. Indicative only.
  • Competitors shown are candidates. The 2 inspirational competitors are not chosen yet.
  • Competitor positions per keyword, AI cited sources and social data are not available yet.
