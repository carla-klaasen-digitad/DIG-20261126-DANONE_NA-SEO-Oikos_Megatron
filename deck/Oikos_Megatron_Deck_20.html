00 · Context and method

Method in 4 steps

The same mechanics as the other holistic studies, applied to the Oikos Pro keyword and prompt set.

1Keyword listing and classification

5,838 keywords, 9 topics, 3 funnel stages

2SEO matrix (SE Ranking)

Share of voice per topic, Google USA

3AI matrix (SE Visible)

Prompts: 70% unbranded, 30% branded

4Benchmark and recommendations

Competitors, SEO and GEO, priorities

SEO and GEO: two views of the same topics

SEO counts where Oikos ranks in Google (SE Ranking share of voice). GEO counts how often Oikos is mentioned in AI answers (SE Visible visibility, share of voice and sentiment). A topic can be won in one and lost in the other.

Reading the slides

Each funnel stage is read the same way: demand and categories, trends, attribute view, SEO competitive environment, AI answer visibility. Blank boxes marked PENDING are data not yet available.
